# Creativity in UX

Creativity is useful for discovering alternative ways to solve user problems, not for adding novelty without purpose.

## Divergent thinking

Generate multiple possible solutions before selecting one.

Techniques:
- How Might We questions
- analogy
- sketching
- rapid ideation
- journey remixing
- constraint-based ideation

## Convergent thinking

Evaluate ideas against:
- user needs
- feasibility
- accessibility
- clarity
- business requirements
- safety
- evidence

## Constraints can help

Useful constraints can focus creativity:
- limited screen space
- low bandwidth
- novice users
- accessibility requirements
- time-sensitive tasks

## Creativity and mental models

A novel interaction may be less usable if users cannot understand it.

When introducing novelty:
- provide clear signifiers
- make behavior discoverable
- preserve familiar concepts where useful
- provide recovery paths

## Creative review

- Does the novelty solve a real problem?
- Can users understand it without excessive explanation?
- Does it work for different abilities and contexts?
- Is there a simpler familiar pattern that performs equally well?
