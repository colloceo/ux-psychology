# Empathy, Emotion, and Trust

## Empathy

Empathy in UX means understanding users' goals, constraints, language, emotions, and context.

It is not the same as assuming you know what users feel.

Use:
- interviews
- observation
- usability testing
- journey mapping
- support-ticket analysis
- contextual research

## Context matters

A flow used:
- on a phone
- under time pressure
- with poor connectivity
- by a first-time user
- after an error

may require different design decisions than the same flow used in a calm desktop setting.

## Emotion

Emotion influences attention, interpretation, motivation, and memory.

Useful emotional design goals include:
- confidence
- clarity
- reassurance
- control
- accomplishment

Avoid intentionally producing:
- shame
- fear
- panic
- confusion
- social pressure

## Trust

Trust is supported by:
- transparency
- predictable behavior
- accurate status
- clear policies
- consistent branding
- credible information
- respectful error handling
- user control

Trust is damaged by:
- hidden charges
- misleading labels
- unexplained permissions
- fake scarcity
- deceptive defaults
- difficult cancellation

## Empathy review

Ask:
- What is the user trying to accomplish?
- What could make them uncertain?
- What constraints are they under?
- What happens if they make a mistake?
- Does the interface preserve dignity and control?
